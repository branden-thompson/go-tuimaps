## Summary

Counted from the rows above: **High 3** · **Medium 14** · **Low 7** · **Closed 2** — 26 risks (RS-23 and RS-24 added after DISCOVER's red-team round 2, RS-25 after its round 3, RS-26 after the PLAN red-team). *(An earlier version of this summary said "Medium 5, Low 8" when its rows gave 4 and 9, and the same error is in the message of commit `8830c11`; this is the correction.)*

The High risks — the overlay contract (RS-2), memory (RS-7) and the first host's readiness (RS-11); asynchronous ownership (RS-4) fell to Medium when HUM LEAD ruled the model (D-73) — are all about PLAN getting the design right inside fixed budgets. Scope and size (RS-5, RS-21) fell to Medium when HUM LEAD ruled a first slice (D-44). None says the idea does not work.

The risk that could have ended the project — that a weather field cannot be read under a braille map — is retired for the case HUM LEAD has seen in HUM LEAD's own terminal (D-32). HUM LEAD has since viewed the whole specimen set (D-41): RS-3 is Low for the braille renderer, and the block renderer's legibility is carried separately as RS-22.
