## Summary

**Fix: 14** (L-1, L-2, L-3, L-5, L-6, L-7, L-8, L-9, L-10, L-11, L-12, L-15, L-16, and L-17g) · **Fix, already ruled: 2** (L-4 by D-12, L-13 by D-27) · **Replicate: L-17 (a)–(f)** · **Not applicable: L-14**.

Every *fix* either changes nothing a user can see, or changes it for the better in a way behavioural parity (D-11) allows. The ones a side-by-side comparison with upstream would show are L-1 (block shapes), L-3 (no stray chords), L-7 (non-Latin label positions) and L-4 (rivers and parks appear).

Nothing here is proposed for replication *as a bug*. The replicated items in L-17 are conventions, not mistakes.

## What this ledger does not cover

Differences that come from rulings rather than from upstream defects are recorded against their parity rows when the matrix is frozen, not here: fresh styles and no renaming shim (D-24, row P-40); colours resolved at draw time (D-26, rows P-41 and P-44); stand-in tiles and parallel fetching (D-30, row P-51); water owning its cells under a field (S1-2, rows P-15 and P-22); the pointer operations (D-17); the PMTiles source (D-18) *[Added 2026-09-18 after red-team round 2:]* the slowed marker flash (D-56, row P-59b since D-61); braille as the default renderer with block opt-in (D-42, row P-04).
